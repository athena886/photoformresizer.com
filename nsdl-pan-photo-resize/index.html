NSDL PAN Photo Resizer – Resize Photo & Signature to Exact Size

Many NSDL PAN applications are returned because the photo or signature is the wrong size, does not have the required DPI, or is over the file size limit. Fixing the files after submission means another upload and more waiting. This tool prepares both images in the exact format before you apply.

How to Resize Photo and Signature for NSDL PAN

  1. Open the Photo tab and upload a recent color photograph. Drag the preview and use the zoom control until the crop looks correct.
  2. Open the Signature tab and upload a clear signature photo. Drag and zoom the preview so the full signature fits inside the frame.
  3. Enter the applicant name if you want it included in the download file names, then download the JPEG files.
  4. For a single download, open Both + ZIP, prepare both images, and use Download ZIP to get the photo and signature together.

Common Mistakes That Get Your Application Rejected

A blurry photo, a strong shadow across the face, the wrong aspect ratio, or a signature that is cut off can all cause a rejection. Uploading a file without the required DPI information can also fail even when the image looks correct on your phone.

Tips to Get It Right the First Time

Use even lighting and a plain, light background. Keep the whole face or full signature visible in the crop tool. Avoid screenshots, heavily edited images, and files that are already compressed to a very small size before processing.

Is This Tool Safe?

Yes. The tool uses the Canvas API in your browser. Your photo and signature are not uploaded, posted, or sent anywhere. All cropping, resizing, DPI writing, compression, and ZIP creation happen on your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the photo size required for NSDL PAN card?

The photograph must be 3.5 (H) × 2.5 (W) cm at 200 DPI. That converts to 197 × 276 px (W × H), in JPEG format, with a maximum file size of 20 KB.

What is the signature size required for NSDL PAN?

The signature must be 2 (H) × 4.5 (W) cm at 200 DPI. That converts to 354 × 158 px (W × H), in JPEG format, with a maximum file size of 10 KB.

What is the maximum file size for NSDL PAN photo and signature?

The photograph can be up to 20 KB and the signature can be up to 10 KB. This tool compresses each JPEG to stay within its limit while keeping the highest practical quality.

My photo looks fine but the portal rejects it. Why?

The image may have the wrong pixel dimensions, aspect ratio, JPEG DPI information, or file size. A visually correct photo can still fail if its metadata does not say 200 DPI. This tool writes the required JFIF density information into the downloaded JPEG.

Can I prepare these images on my phone?

Yes. The page is designed for mobile browsers and works with photos selected from an Android or iPhone. Keep the page open while the browser processes the images locally.

What is the difference between NSDL and UTI PAN requirements?

NSDL and UTI are different PAN service providers, so their portals can present application instructions separately. Always verify the current instructions for the provider you are using. If your application uses UTI, see the UTI PAN photo resizer page when it is available.
