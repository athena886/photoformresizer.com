Driving Licence Photo Resizer – Resize Photo & Signature Online

Driving licence applications can be delayed when the uploaded photo or signature does not match the required dimensions, DPI, or file size. This free tool prepares both images in your browser, with no upload and no account required.

How to Resize Photo and Signature for Driving Licence

  1. Open the Photo tab and upload a recent passport-style colour photograph. Drag the preview and use Zoom to centre your face.
  2. Open the Signature tab and upload a signature made with black pen on white paper. Adjust the crop until the full signature fits.
  3. Enter the applicant name if you want it included in the download file names, then download the JPEG files.
  4. For one download containing both files, open Both + ZIP, prepare both images, and select Download ZIP.

Common Mistakes That Get Your Driving Licence Photo Rejected

A blurry image, a dark or patterned background, a cropped head, or a signature that is unclear can cause problems. Using a screenshot or an image that is already below the minimum file size can also make the upload unacceptable.

Tips to Get It Right the First Time

Face the camera directly with a relaxed expression and use even lighting. Wear no cap or dark glasses, and make sure normal glasses do not reflect light. Sign only on white paper so the signature remains clear after compression.

Is This Tool Safe?

Yes. The browser performs the crop, resize, DPI metadata update, compression, and ZIP creation locally. Your photograph and signature are not uploaded, stored, or sent to any server.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the photo size for a driving licence?

The official preferred photograph size is 420 × 525 px (W × H), equivalent to 35 × 45 mm. This tool also writes 300 DPI into the JPEG.

What is the signature size for a driving licence?

The official preferred signature size is 256 × 64 px (W × H). The signature should be made with a black pen on white paper.

What is the maximum file size for a driving licence photo and signature?

The official guidance states 10–20 KB for both the photograph and the signature. This tool keeps each JPEG within that range, up to the stated maximum.

What DPI should a driving licence photo be?

The official scanning guidance specifies a minimum of 300 DPI. This tool writes a 300 DPI JFIF density header into the output JPEG.

Can I use a photo taken with my phone?

Yes, if the image is clear and meets the portrait requirements. Upload the phone photo, then use the crop and zoom controls to match the preferred dimensions.

Can I download the photo and signature together?

Yes. Open Both + ZIP, upload both images, adjust each crop, and download one ZIP containing the photo and signature JPEG files.

Does this tool upload my photo anywhere?

No. All processing happens inside your browser. The tool does not send your photograph or signature to a server.
